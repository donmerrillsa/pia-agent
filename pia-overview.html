What PIA Does

Continuous Pipeline Monitoring Connects to your HubSpot pipeline and keeps watch automatically — no manual pulling of reports.
Stalled Deal Detection Flags deals going quiet at each stage, using configurable inactivity thresholds (7/10/14 days).
Confidence Scoring Know whether this month's forecast is real or wishful thinking, based on how much of your pipeline is stalled.
Daily Pulse A short email each morning (Mon–Thu) showing what changed in your pipeline overnight.
Weekly Pipeline Integrity Report The full picture, every Friday — stalled value, confidence trend, and what needs attention.
Failure Alerts If the monitoring itself breaks, you're told immediately — not left in the dark.

What PIA Doesn't Do — On Purpose

  • Doesn't write anything back into your CRM
  • Doesn't draft outreach or follow-up messages
  • Doesn't run approval workflows or task assignment
  • Doesn't predict win probability or replace your forecast process
  • Doesn't decide what action to take next — that's still you and your team
PIA watches the pipeline and flags drift before it becomes invisible. It doesn't decide what to do about it — that boundary is what keeps it fast, reliable, and easy to trust.
